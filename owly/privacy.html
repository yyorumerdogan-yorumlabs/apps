Owly: Debt Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Owly ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect your email address (for account creation and login) and the debt records you create within the app (amounts, names, due dates). This data is stored securely using Supabase.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the app's core functionality: tracking debts and sending reminder notifications. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for data storage and RevenueCat for subscription management. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Data Deletion
